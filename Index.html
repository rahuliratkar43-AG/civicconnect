HOW IT WORKS

Report Civic Issues in Four Simple Steps

A simple digital process designed to make civic reporting easier for everyone.

Identify the Issue

Find a civic problem such as a pothole, garbage, water leakage or broken streetlight.

02

Upload the issue details, photograph and location through the reporting form.

03

Track Your Complaint

Use your complaint ID to check the current status of your submitted issue.

The concerned department can process the complaint and update its resolution status.
